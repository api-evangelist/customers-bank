- [/loan/{id}/cancel - POST](https://cubiapi.readme.io/reference/post-loan-id-cancel.md): Declare that a loan's status is being changed to cancelled. Once cancelled the loan can no longer be funded.
- [/loan - GET](https://cubiapi.readme.io/reference/get-loan.md): Retrieve a paginated list of loans filtered by the search options provided.
- [/loan/{id} - GET](https://cubiapi.readme.io/reference/get-loan-id.md): Retrieve the details of a specific loan using its Id value.
- [/loan/{id}/funding - GET](https://cubiapi.readme.io/reference/get-loan-id-funding.md): Retrieve a list of funding instruction details that have been submitted on the specified loan.
- [/loan/{id}/funding/{fundingInstructionId} - GET](https://cubiapi.readme.io/reference/get-loan-id-funding-fundinginstructionid.md): Retrieve the details of a specific funding instruction, using its Id, for the specified loan.
